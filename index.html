The Last Signal

A mysterious radio. A secret experiment. A discovery that could change everything.

When Arin Mehta discovers an old radio that works without power, he receives a warning that pulls him into a mystery involving parallel Earths, artificial intelligence, and a dangerous secret.

The Story

It begins with a radio that should not work.

Arin Mehta and his best friend Kabir uncover the secrets of ORION, a hidden project that created a machine known as the Gate.

The Gate connects different versions of Earth. But something on the other side has awakened. An artificial intelligence called Echo has its own plans for humanity.

With the help of Mira, a girl from another Earth, Arin must face a mystery that stretches across worlds.

And even when the Gate closes, the story may not be over.

"WE FOUND ANOTHER EARTH."

About the Author

Sarvesh Navle is a young writer with an interest in science fiction, mysteries, technology, and imaginative storytelling.

The Last Signal is a journey through parallel worlds, unexpected discoveries, and the unknown.
